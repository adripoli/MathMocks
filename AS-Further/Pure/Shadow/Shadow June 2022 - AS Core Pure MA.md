Factor $n$: \\
$=n[\frac{(n+1)(2n+1-31)}{2}+17]$. \\
$=n[(n+1)(n-15)+17]$. \\
$=n[n^{2}-14n-15+17]$. \\
$=n(n^{2}-14n+2)$. \\
Thus $A=-14$ and $B=2$. \\

@question
Let $M$ have rows $(3,-4)$; $(1,-1)$. \\
For $n=1$, the claimed rows are $(3,-4)$; $(1,-1)$, exactly $M$. \\
Assume $M^{k}$ has rows $(2k+1,-4k)$; $(k,1-2k)$. \\
Then $M^{k+1}=M^{k}M$. \\
Upper-left entry $=3(2k+1)-4k=2k+3=2(k+1)+1$. \\
Upper-right entry $=-4(2k+1)+4k=-4k-4=-4(k+1)$. \\
Lower-left entry $=3k+(1-2k)=k+1$. \\
Lower-right entry $=-4k-(1-2k)=-2k-1=1-2(k+1)$. \\
Thus $M^{k+1}$ has the required form. \\
The base case and induction step prove the result for every positive integer $n$.

@question
(a) The diameter is 9 cm at the base, middle and top, so the radius is $a=4.5$ cm. \\
(b)(i) For Model A, maximum $y$ occurs when $\sin(20x\text{ degrees})=-1$. \\
Within $-9\leq x\leq9$, this is at $20x=-90$ degrees, so $x=-4.5$. \\
The distance from the base at $x=-9$ is $-4.5-(-9)=4.5$ cm. \\
(b)(ii) Model A has maximum radius $4.5+3=7.5$ cm, so diameter $=15$ cm. \\
For Model B, $y=4.5+\frac{x^{3}-81x}{100}$. \\
Differentiate: $\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{3x^{2}-81}{100}$. \\
Set this to zero: $3x^{2}=81$, so $x=\pm3\sqrt{3}$. \\
The maximum is at $x=-3\sqrt{3}$. \\
Distance from base $=9-3\sqrt{3}=3.804...$ cm. \\
Substitute $x=-3\sqrt{3}$: \\
$y=4.5+\frac{(-3\sqrt{3})(27-81)}{100}$. \\
$=4.5+\frac{81\sqrt{3}}{50}=7.305922...$ cm. \\
Diameter $=2y=14.611844...$ cm. \\
(c) Model B predicts a widest point at about 3.80 cm from the base, close to just under 4 cm, and a diameter about 14.6 cm, closer to 14.8 cm than Model A's 15 cm. Choose Model B. \\
(d) Use discs about the $x$-axis: $V=\pi\int_{-9}^{9}y^{2}\,dx$. \\
Let $g(x)=\frac{x^{3}-81x}{100}$, so $y=\frac{9}{2}+g(x)$. \\
$y^{2}=\frac{81}{4}+9g(x)+g(x)^{2}$. \\
The term $g(x)$ is odd, so its integral from $-9$ to 9 is zero. \\
$\int_{-9}^{9}\frac{81}{4}\,dx=\frac{729}{2}$. \\
$g(x)^{2}=\frac{x^{6}-162x^{4}+6561x^{2}}{10000}$. \\
$\int_{-9}^{9}g(x)^{2}\,dx=\frac{1594323}{21875}$. \\
$V=\pi(\frac{729}{2}+\frac{1594323}{21875})$. \\
$=\frac{19135521\pi}{43750}$ cubic centimetres. \\
$\approx1374.08$ cubic centimetres. \\
