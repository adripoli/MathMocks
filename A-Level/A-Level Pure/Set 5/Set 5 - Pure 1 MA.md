Cancel $u$: \\
$= \int_{1}^{9} \frac{1}{(1 + u)^{3}\ln 3} \, du$ \\
$= \frac{1}{\ln 3}\int_{1}^{9} \frac{1}{(1 + u)^{3}} \, du$ \\

$\int_{0}^{2} \frac{3^{x+2}}{(1 + 3^{x})^{3}} \, dx = 9\int_{0}^{2} \frac{3^{x}}{(1 + 3^{x})^{3}} \, dx$ \\
Use (a): \\

$= \frac{9}{\ln 3}\left( -\frac{1}{200} + \frac{25}{200} \right)$ \\
$= \frac{9}{\ln 3} \times \frac{24}{200}$ \\
$= \frac{9}{\ln 3} \times \frac{3}{25}$ \\
