## Question 9

$$y = ab^t$$

where $a$ and $b$ are constants.

**(a)** Show that this equation can be written in the form

$$\log_{10} y = t \log_{10} b + c$$

expressing the constant $c$ in terms of $a$.

**(2 marks)**

A doctor measures the amount of antibiotic in the patient's bloodstream at regular intervals for the first 5 hours. She plots a graph of $\log_{10} y$ against $t$ and finds that the points lie close to a straight line passing through $(0, 2.23)$ with gradient $-0.076$.

**(b)** Estimate, to 2 significant figures, the value of $a$ and the value of $b$.

**(2 marks)**

With reference to this model:

**(c)**
- **(i)** Give a practical interpretation of the value of the constant $a$.
- **(ii)** Give a practical interpretation of the value of the constant $b$.

**(2 marks)**

**(d)** Use the model to estimate the time taken, after the antibiotic was first given, for the amount of antibiotic in the patient's bloodstream to fall to $30$ milligrams. Give your answer, in hours, correct to one decimal place.

**(2 marks)**

**(e)** Comment on the reliability of your estimate in part (d).

**(1 mark)**

**(Total for Question 9 is 9 marks)**

## Question 10

A biologist conducted an experiment to investigate the growth of mould on a slice of bread. The biologist measured the surface area of bread, $A \text{ cm}^2$, covered by mould at times, $t$ days, after the start of the experiment.

Initially $9.00 \text{ cm}^2$ of the bread was covered by mould and 6 days later, $56.25 \text{ cm}^2$ of the bread was covered by mould.

In the biologist's model, the rate of increase of the surface area of bread covered by mould, at any time $t$ days, is proportional to the square root of that area.

**(a)** By forming and solving a differential equation, show that the biologist's model leads to the equation

$$A = \left(\frac{3}{4}t + 3\right)^2$$

**(6 marks)**

The biologist's full set of results are shown in Table 1:

| $t$ (days) | 0 | 6 | 12 | 18 | 24 | 30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $A \text{ (cm}^2\text{)}$ | 9.00 | 56.25 | 143.78 | 271.19 | 334.81 | 337.33 |

Use the last four measurements from Table 1 to:

**(b)**
- **(i)** Evaluate the biologist's model.
- **(ii)** Suggest a possible explanation of the results.

**(3 marks)**

**(Total for Question 10 is 9 marks)**
