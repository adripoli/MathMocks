@q 1
@topic Venn diagram, independent events
@marks 8
@question
(a) Mutually exclusive events cannot happen together, so their circles do not overlap \\
$S$ is inside $P$ and does not touch $Q$ or $R$, and $P$ does not overlap $R$ \\
$P$ and $R$ do not overlap, so $P$ and $R$ are mutually exclusive \\
(Other pairs: $S$ and $Q$, or $S$ and $R$) \\
(b) The probabilities in $Q$ add up to $P(Q) = 0.5$ \\
$Q$ is made of three parts: $Q$ with $P$ (0.36), $Q$ only (0.09), and $Q$ with $R$ ($a$) \\
$0.36 + 0.09 + a = 0.5$ \\
Collect the numbers: $0.36 + 0.09 = 0.45$ \\
$0.45 + a = 0.5$ \\
Subtract 0.45: \\
$a = 0.05$ \\
(c) $P$ and $Q$ are independent, so $P(P \text{ and } Q) = P(P) \times P(Q)$ \\
$P(P \text{ and } Q) = 0.36$ and $P(Q) = 0.5$ \\
$P(P) \times 0.5 = 0.36$ \\
Divide by 0.5: \\
$P(P) = 0.72$ \\
$P$ is made of three parts: $P$ only ($b$), $S$ (0.2) and $P$ with $Q$ (0.36) \\
$b + 0.2 + 0.36 = 0.72$ \\
$b + 0.56 = 0.72$ \\
Subtract 0.56: \\
$b = 0.16$ \\
(d)(i) $P(Q' | R) = \frac{P(Q' \text{ and } R)}{P(R)}$ \\
$Q'$ and $R$ is the part of $R$ not in $Q$, which has probability $c$ \\
$R$ is made of $c$ and the part with $Q$, which is $a = 0.05$ \\
$P(R) = c + 0.05$ \\
$\frac{c}{c + 0.05} = 0.6$ \\
Multiply both sides by $(c + 0.05)$: \\
$c = 0.6(c + 0.05)$ \\
Expand the bracket: \\
$c = 0.6c + 0.03$ \\
Subtract $0.6c$: \\
$0.4c = 0.03$ \\
Divide by 0.4: \\
$c = 0.075$ \\
(ii) All the probabilities add up to 1 \\
Probability inside $P$: 0.72 \\
$Q$ only: 0.09 \\
$R$ (with and without $Q$): $0.05 + 0.075 = 0.125$ \\
$0.72 + 0.09 + 0.125 + d = 1$ \\
$0.935 + d = 1$ \\
Subtract 0.935: \\
$d = 0.065$

(c) Priti believes there is a correlation, which could be positive or negative, so the test is two tailed \\
$H_{0}: \text{rho} = 0$ \\
$H_{1}: \text{rho} \neq 0$ \\
Sample size $n = 25$ and the significance level is 5% \\
Each tail has 2.5%, so use the two tail 5% critical value for $n = 25$: \\
Critical values are $\pm 0.3961$ \\
The critical region is $r < -0.3961$ or $r > 0.3961$ \\
The sample value is $r = -0.377$ \\
$-0.377$ is between $-0.3961$ and 0.3961, so it is not in the critical region \\
Do not reject $H_{0}$ \\
There is no evidence, at the 5% level, of a correlation between Daily Total Sunshine and Daily Maximum Relative Humidity \\
(d) A humidity of 95% is high \\

@q 3
@topic Box plot, mean, st dev, outlier
@marks 10
@question
(a) Range $=$ highest value $-$ lowest value \\
From the box plot the highest is 408 and the lowest is 42 \\
Range $= 408 - 42$ \\
Range $= 366$ minutes \\
(b) Interquartile range $= Q_{3} - Q_{1}$ \\
From the box plot $Q_{3} = 150$ and $Q_{1} = 84$ \\
$IQR = 150 - 84$ \\
$IQR = 66$ minutes \\
(c) Mean $= \frac{\sum x}{n}$ \\
$= \frac{3850}{35}$ \\
$= 110$ minutes \\
(d) Standard deviation: $\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{\sum x^{2}}{n} - \text{mean}^{2}}$ \\
$\frac{\sum x^{2}}{n} = \frac{575960}{35}$ \\
$= 16456$ \\
$\text{mean}^{2} = 110^{2} = 12100$ \\
$\sigma = \sqrt{16456 - 12100}$ \\
$\sigma = \sqrt{4356}$ \\
$\sigma = 66$ minutes \\
(e) An outlier is a value more than 3 standard deviations above the mean \\
Mean $+ 3\sigma = 110 + 3 \times 66$ \\
$3 \times 66 = 198$ \\
$110 + 198 = 308$ \\
Only values above 308 are outliers \\
On the plot, the maximum 408 is above 308, and the only other value marked beyond the whisker is not above 308 \\
So there is only one outlier \\
(f) The two new times are $a$ and $b$ with $a > b$ \\
With 35 values, the median is the 18th value, which is 120 \\
For the median to decrease with 37 values, both new times must be below 120 \\
For the mean not to change, the two new times must have the same mean as the old data, 110 \\
$\frac{a + b}{2} = 110$ \\
Multiply by 2: \\
$a + b = 220$ \\
Choose two values, both less than 120, that add to 220 and have $a > b$: \\
Try $a = 115$ and $b = 105$ \\
$115 + 105 = 220$, which keeps the mean at 110 \\
$115 < 120$ and $105 < 120$, so the median decreases \\
$115 > 105$, so $a > b$ \\
So $a = 115$ and $b = 105$ is a possible choice \\
(g) The new times 115 and 105 are both within 1 standard deviation of the mean (within $110 \pm 66$) \\
Values close to the mean make the spread smaller, so the standard deviation of all 37 values will be lower than 66

@q 4
@topic Discrete random variable, sum, angles
@marks 10
@question
(a) The probabilities add up to 1: \\
$\frac{c}{5} + \frac{c}{10} + \frac{c}{15} + \frac{c}{20} + \frac{c}{25} = 1$ \\
Use a common denominator of 300: \\
$\frac{c}{5} = \frac{60c}{300}$ \\
$\frac{c}{10} = \frac{30c}{300}$ \\
$\frac{c}{15} = \frac{20c}{300}$ \\
$\frac{c}{20} = \frac{15c}{300}$ \\
$\frac{c}{25} = \frac{12c}{300}$ \\
$\frac{60c + 30c + 20c + 15c + 12c}{300} = 1$ \\
Add the numerators: $60 + 30 + 20 + 15 + 12 = 137$ \\
$\frac{137c}{300} = 1$ \\
Multiply by 300 and divide by 137: \\
$c = \frac{300}{137}$ as required \\
(b) The ways to get $D_{1} + D_{2} = 40$ with each value from 5, 10, 15, 20, 25: \\
$25 + 15$, $15 + 25$ and $20 + 20$ \\
Probability of $D_{1} = 25$ and $D_{2} = 15$: $\frac{c}{25} \times \frac{c}{15}$ \\
The case $D_{1} = 15$ and $D_{2} = 25$ has the same probability \\
Probability of $D_{1} = 20$ and $D_{2} = 20$: $\frac{c}{20} \times \frac{c}{20}$ \\
$P(D_{1} + D_{2} = 40) = 2 \times \frac{c}{25} \times \frac{c}{15} + \left( \frac{c}{20} \right)^{2}$ \\
$c = \frac{300}{137} = 2.18978$ \\
$\frac{c}{25} = 0.087591$ \\
$\frac{c}{15} = 0.145985$ \\
$\frac{c}{20} = 0.109489$ \\
$2 \times 0.087591 \times 0.145985 = 0.025572$ \\
$0.109489^{2} = 0.011988$ \\
$P(D_{1} + D_{2} = 40) = 0.025572 + 0.011988$ \\
$= 0.03756$ \\
$= 0.0376$ to 3 significant figures \\
(c) The first four terms of an arithmetic sequence with first term $a$ and common difference $d$ are $a$, $a + d$, $a + 2d$, $a + 3d$ \\
These are the angles of a quadrilateral, so they add up to 360 degrees: \\
$a + (a + d) + (a + 2d) + (a + 3d) = 360$ \\
Collect like terms: \\
$4a + 6d = 360$ \\
Divide by 2: \\
$2a + 3d = 180$ \\
Make $a$ the subject: \\
$a = \frac{180 - 3d}{2}$ \\
The smallest angle is $a$ (since $d > 0$) and we need $a > 65$ \\
Try each possible value of $d$: \\
$d = 5$: $a = \frac{180 - 15}{2} = 82.5$, which is more than 65 \\
$d = 10$: $a = \frac{180 - 30}{2} = 75$, which is more than 65 \\
$d = 15$: $a = \frac{180 - 45}{2} = 67.5$, which is more than 65 \\
$d = 20$: $a = \frac{180 - 60}{2} = 60$, which is not more than 65 \\
$d = 25$: $a = \frac{180 - 75}{2} = 52.5$, which is not more than 65 \\
So $d$ must be 5, 10 or 15 \\
$P(D = 5 \text{ or } 10 \text{ or } 15) = \frac{c}{5} + \frac{c}{10} + \frac{c}{15}$ \\
Common denominator 30: $\frac{6c}{30} + \frac{3c}{30} + \frac{2c}{30}$ \\
$= \frac{11c}{30}$ \\
Put in $c = \frac{300}{137}$: \\
$\frac{11}{30} \times \frac{300}{137}$ \\
Cancel 30 into 300: \\
$= \frac{11 \times 10}{137}$ \\
$= \frac{110}{137}$

@q 5
@topic Normal model, hypothesis test, truncation
@marks 15
@question
(a) Let $X$ be the time spent, $X$ follows $N(25, 12^{2})$ \\
Standardise 40: $z = \frac{40 - 25}{12}$ \\
$z = 1.25$ \\
$P(X > 40) = P(Z > 1.25)$ \\
$= 1 - 0.8944$ \\
$= 0.1056$ \\
$P(X > 40) = 0.106$ \\
(b) The patients complain that the mean is more than 25, so the test is one tailed \\
$H_{0}: \mu = 25$ \\
$H_{1}: \mu > 25$ \\
Under $H_{0}$ the mean of a sample of 30 follows: \\
$\text{X-bar}$ follows $N\left( 25, \frac{12^{2}}{30} \right)$ \\
Standard error: $\frac{12}{\sqrt{30}} = 2.1909$ \\
Find the probability of a sample mean of 28 or more: \\
$z = \frac{28 - 25}{2.1909}$ \\
$z = 1.3693$ \\
$P(\text{X-bar} > 28) = P(Z > 1.3693)$ \\
$= 0.0855$ \\
The $p$-value is 0.0855 \\
$0.0855 > 0.05$, so the result is not significant: do not reject $H_{0}$ \\
There is no evidence, at the 5% level, to support the patients' complaint \\
(c)(i) $T$ follows $N(4, 2.5^{2})$ \\
$z = \frac{3 - 4}{2.5}$ \\
$z = -0.4$ \\
$P(T < 3) = P(Z < -0.4)$ \\
$P(T < 3) = 0.3446$ \\
$P(T < 3) = 0.345$ \\
(ii) Conditional probability: $P(T < 3 | T > 0) = \frac{P(0 < T < 3)}{P(T > 0)}$ \\
Find $P(T > 0)$: $z = \frac{0 - 4}{2.5} = -1.6$ \\
$P(T < 0) = P(Z < -1.6) = 0.0548$ \\
$P(T > 0) = 1 - 0.0548$ \\
$P(T > 0) = 0.9452$ \\
Find $P(0 < T < 3)$: \\
$P(0 < T < 3) = P(T < 3) - P(T < 0)$ \\
$= 0.3446 - 0.0548$ \\
$= 0.2898$ \\
$P(T < 3 | T > 0) = \frac{0.2898}{0.9452}$ \\
$= 0.3066$ \\
$P(T < 3 | T > 0) = 0.307$ \\
(iii) A time cannot be negative \\
But the model gives $P(T < 0) = 0.0548$, which is about 5.5%, a probability that is not negligible \\
So this normal distribution may not be a good model for $T$ \\
(d) The new model only has $T > 3$ \\
The median $t$ is the value where half of the values above 3 are below it: \\
$P(T < t | T > 3) = 0.5$ \\
Equivalently $P(T > t | T > 3) = 0.5$ \\
$P(T > t | T > 3) = \frac{P(T > t)}{P(T > 3)}$ \\
$\frac{P(T > t)}{P(T > 3)} = 0.5$ \\
Find $P(T > 3)$: \\
$P(T > 3) = 1 - 0.3446$ \\
$P(T > 3) = 0.6554$ \\
Multiply both sides by 0.6554: \\
$P(T > t) = 0.5 \times 0.6554$ \\
$P(T > t) = 0.3277$ \\
So $P(T < t) = 1 - 0.3277 = 0.6723$ \\
Find the $z$ value with 0.6723 below it: $z = 0.4468$ \\
$\frac{t - 4}{2.5} = 0.4468$ \\
Multiply by 2.5: \\
$t - 4 = 1.1170$ \\
Add 4: \\
$t = 5.117$ \\
$t = 5.1$ minutes to 1 decimal place
