First term: $\sin \frac{\theta}{4} \approx \frac{\theta}{4}$ \\
$40\sin \frac{\theta}{4} \approx 40 \times \frac{\theta}{4}$ \\
$40\sin \frac{\theta}{4} \approx 10\theta$ \\

Square both sides: \\
$\cos^{2} \theta \approx \left( 1 - \frac{\theta^{2}}{2} \right)^{2}$ \\
$\cos^{2} \theta \approx 1 - \theta^{2} + \frac{\theta^{4}}{4}$ \\
$\theta$ is small, so $\theta^{4}$ is tiny and can be ignored: \\
$\cos^{2} \theta \approx 1 - \theta^{2}$ \\

$5\cos^{2} \theta \approx 5 - 5\theta^{2}$ \\
Add the two parts: \\
$40\sin \frac{\theta}{4} + 5\cos^{2} \theta \approx 10\theta + 5 - 5\theta^{2}$ \\

@question

$\sin (180n - 90) = \sin 180n\cos 90 - \cos 180n\sin 90$ \\
$\cos 90 = 0$ and $\sin 90 = 1$ \\
$\sin (180n - 90) = -\cos 180n$ \\

$\sin (180n - 90) = -(-1)^{n}$ \\
Each term of the series is: \\

Common ratio: $\frac{64}{125} \div \left( -\frac{16}{25} \right) = -\frac{4}{5}$ \\
$|r| = \frac{4}{5} < 1$, so the series converges \\

$S_{\infty} = \frac{-\frac{16}{25}}{1 - \left( -\frac{4}{5} \right)}$ \\
$S_{\infty} = \frac{-\frac{16}{25}}{\frac{9}{5}}$ \\

$S_{\infty} = -\frac{16}{25} \times \frac{5}{9}$ \\
$S_{\infty} = -\frac{80}{225}$ \\
