@q 13
@topic Cheesecake temperature model
@marks 12
@question
A cheesecake is taken out of the fridge and placed in the oven. The internal temperature, $T$ degrees C, of the cheesecake, $t$ minutes after it was placed in the oven, is modelled by the equation \\
$T = 80 - Ae^{-kt}$ \\
where $A$ and $k$ are positive constants. \\
Given that \\
- exactly 10 minutes after it was placed in the oven, the internal temperature of the cheesecake was 33 degrees C \\
- exactly 30 minutes after it was placed in the oven, the internal temperature of the cheesecake was 65 degrees C \\
(a) find a complete equation for $T$ in terms of $t$, giving the value of each of $A$ and $k$ to 3 significant figures. (4) \\
Use the equation of the model to answer parts (b), (c) and (d). \\
According to the model, there is a limit to the internal temperature of this cheesecake. \\
(b) Deduce this limit. (1) \\
(c)(i) Find the initial internal temperature of the cheesecake. \\
(ii) Hence explain whether this is a suitable model for the initial internal temperature of the cheesecake. (2) \\
(d)(i) Find $\frac{dT}{dt}$, giving your answer in simplest form. (2) \\
(ii) Hence find the value of $t$ for which the internal temperature of the cheesecake is increasing at a rate of 2 degrees C per minute. (3)
