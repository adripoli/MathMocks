$\tan x \cos x - \sin x \cos^{2} x = \frac{\sin x + \cos^{3} x}{\tan x}$ \\

$\tan^{2} x - \tan x - 1 = 0$ (3) \\

(c) Hence find the number of solutions of the equation \\
$\tan \frac{1}{2}x \cos \frac{1}{2}x - \sin \frac{1}{2}x \cos^{2} \frac{1}{2}x = \frac{\sin \frac{1}{2}x + \cos^{3} \frac{1}{2}x}{\tan \frac{1}{2}x}$ \\
in the interval $-900 < x < 900$ degrees, explaining briefly the reason for your answer. (2)
