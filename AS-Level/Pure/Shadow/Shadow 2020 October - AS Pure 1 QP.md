@q 8
@topic Exponential cooling model
@marks 9
@question
The temperature, $\theta$ degrees C, of a cup of tea $t$ minutes after it was placed on a table in a room, is modelled by the equation \\
$\theta = 16 + 69e^{-\frac{t}{6}}$, $t \geq 0$ \\
Find, according to the model, \\
(a) the temperature of the cup of tea when it was placed on the table, (1) \\
(b) the value of $t$, to two decimal places, when the temperature of the cup of tea was 40 degrees C. (3) \\
(c) Explain why, according to this model, the temperature of the cup of tea could not fall to 10 degrees C. (1) \\
The temperature, $\mu$ degrees C, of a second cup of tea $t$ minutes after it was placed on a table in a different room, is modelled by the equation \\
$\mu = A + Be^{-\frac{t}{6}}$, $t \geq 0$ \\
where $A$ and $B$ are constants. \\
Figure 2 shows a sketch of $\mu$ against $t$ with two data points that lie on the curve. \\
The line $l$, also shown on Figure 2, is the asymptote to the curve. \\
Using the equation of this model and the information given in Figure 2 \\
(d) find an equation for the asymptote $l$. (4) \\
(Diagram: Figure 2 shows a curve of $\mu$ against $t$ that falls and flattens out towards the horizontal asymptote $l$ above the $t$-axis. The points $(0, 98)$ and $(6, 55)$ lie on the curve.)

is used to model the total number of views of the advert, $V$, in the first $t$ days after the advert went live. \\
(a) Show that $V = ab^{t}$ where $a$ and $b$ are constants to be found. \\
Give the value of $a$ to the nearest whole number and give the value of $b$ to 3 significant figures. (4) \\
(b) Interpret, with reference to the model, the value of $ab$. (1) \\
Using this model, calculate \\
(c) the total number of views of the advert in the first 15 days after the advert went live. \\
Give your answer to 2 significant figures. (2)
